# DeskDash Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

DeskDash is a macOS app that shows your Apple Reminders and Calendar on your desktop.

**DeskDash does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.**

- **Reminders and calendar events** are read and changed only through Apple's EventKit framework, on your Mac. They stay in Apple's own Reminders and Calendar databases and sync to your other devices through the accounts you have set up (for example iCloud). DeskDash keeps no copy of its own.
- **No network access.** DeskDash has no permission to connect to the internet and makes no network requests. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking** of any kind.
- **Settings** (such as window mode, transparency and which calendars to show) are stored locally on your Mac using standard macOS preferences.
- **Permissions.** macOS asks for your permission before DeskDash can access Calendars or Reminders. You can turn access off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/hungnv26/deskdash/issues.
