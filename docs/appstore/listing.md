## Description

DeskDash puts your Apple Reminders and Calendar side by side on your Mac desktop, so the day ahead is always one glance away.

DESKTOP OR WINDOW
• Desktop mode turns DeskDash into a transparent layer on your wallpaper, behind all your apps. Buttons fade away until you need them.
• Window mode shows it as a normal window in front of everything. Switch any time with one click or ⇧⌘D.
• Adjust transparency from solid to clear; text always stays sharp and readable.

REMINDERS
• See every list at once, or focus on one.
• Grouped into Overdue, Today, Upcoming, Later and No due date.
• Add reminders with an optional date and time, edit notes and priority, and tick them off.
• Times you set add an alert, just like the Reminders app.

CALENDAR
• Month grid and day agenda, with your calendar colours.
• Add and edit events, including all-day events and repeats (daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, yearly).
• Read-only calendars and invitations from others are clearly marked.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works directly with the Reminders and Calendar already on your Mac, including iCloud, Google and Exchange accounts. Changes sync to your iPhone and iPad through those accounts.
• No account, no sign-in, no network access, no tracking. Your data never leaves Apple's own apps.

## Notes for App Review

DeskDash shows the Calendar and Reminders already set up on this Mac. On first launch, click "Allow Calendar Access…" and "Allow Reminders Access…" and accept the system prompts; DeskDash needs full access because it can add and edit events and reminders (it never deletes either).

The app opens as a normal window. The icon next to Settings (top right) switches to Desktop mode, a transparent window placed behind other apps on the desktop; the same icon, ⇧⌘D, or the menu-bar icon › "Return to Normal Window" switches back.

Reminders that have a location-based alert are shown read-only, because saving them through EventKit currently crashes on macOS 27; clicking one opens the Reminders app instead.

No sign-in is needed. The app has no network access.
